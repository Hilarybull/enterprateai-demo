Hiring Decision

Should you hire a new team member?

Impact Analysis

  • ↓Cash runway reduces: 8 → 5 months
  • ↓Break even shifts by +2 months
  • ⚠Risk increases under low revenue scenarios
  • ↓Monthly burn rate increases by £3,500

Recommendation

Delay hiring by 2 months or use a contractor to preserve cashflow stability. Safe to hire from Month 3.

Pricing Decision

Planning to raise your prices?

Impact Analysis

  • ↑Revenue increases by +12%
  • ↑Profit margin improves by +18%
  • ↑Remains profitable with up to 8% churn
  • ↑Break even improves by 1.5 months

Recommendation

Increase pricing by 15%. Revenue remains positive even under moderate churn. Implement with a 30 day notice period.

Cost Reduction

Thinking of cutting marketing spend?

Impact Analysis

  • ↑Immediate cashflow improves by £2,100/mo
  • ⚠Pipeline impact: −22% leads by Month 3
  • ↓Revenue dip: −£6,400 by Month 5
  • ⚠Net effect negative beyond Month 4

Recommendation

Do not cut marketing budget. Short term saving creates a revenue hole in Month 4 to 6. Instead, reallocate £800/mo from offline to digital for better ROI.
