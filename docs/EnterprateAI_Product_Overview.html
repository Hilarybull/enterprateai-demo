Platform Overview

What is EnterprateAI?

EnterprateAI is a Business Operating System (BOS) for UK small businesses. It replaces a fragmented stack of disconnected tools with a single integrated workspace where business data is entered once and reused automatically across every module: planning, operations, sales, and decision-making. The platform is AI-powered throughout, using an AI credit system to run intelligent features on demand.

The Problem It Solves

  • Business data entered repeatedly across multiple tools
  • High cost of maintaining a fragmented software stack
  • Decisions made without data, simulation, or risk insight
  • No unified view of business health, risk, or performance
  • Professional documents take hours to create from scratch

Who It Is Built For

  • Founders and early-stage startups (UK)
  • Freelancers and independent consultants
  • Service-based small businesses
  • Suppliers and product businesses
  • Marketing agencies and professional service firms
  • Small businesses seeking investors or funding

Idea Validation

The Validation Wizard takes a business or service idea through a multi-stage structured assessment, using AI to evaluate viability, market fit, revenue potential, and risk, producing a clear accept or reject decision that feeds every other module in the platform.

Validation Pathways 2 Paths

Business Idea Validation
  • Business name and type classification
  • Industry and sector selection
  • Location, currency, and country setup
  • Problem statement and solution description
  • Target market definition
  • Revenue model and pricing inputs
  • Team composition and founder details
  • Competitor analysis fields
  • Initial financial projections
  • Services offered (multi-select)
  • AI field suggestion on every field
  • Stage-default persistence across sessions
Service Validation
  • Service-specific validation pathway
  • Service name and description
  • Target client type and market
  • Delivery frequency (daily / weekly / monthly / yearly / custom)
  • Pricing and revenue model
  • Competitive positioning
  • Accept / reject with scoring breakdown

AI Output and Scoring

  • Viability score (0-100) with band classification
  • Market fit assessment
  • Revenue potential signal
  • Risk flags and mitigation notes
  • Accept or Reject recommendation with reasoning
  • Market data refresh: re-runs live intelligence on accepted validation
  • PDF export of full validation insight report
Cross-module data flow: Once a validation is accepted, its data (business name, sector, services, pricing, team size, etc.) pre-populates all downstream modules: blueprints, simulation, catalogue, and financials, eliminating repeated data entry.

Decision Simulation: Scenario Intelligence & Fragility

The Simulation module is the most powerful intelligence layer of the platform. It combines real-time financial data from the workspace with AI engines to produce scenario projections, risk signals, fragility scores, and strategic recommendations, before any decision is acted upon.

Adaptive Scenario Intelligence (ASI) Auto

ASI automatically monitors the workspace financial snapshot and runs in the background. It detects changes in the business state and updates intelligence without requiring user input.

  • Auto-triggered projection run ("Do nothing" baseline)
  • Auto-detected risk signals from financial state
  • Strategic recommendations tailored to business context
  • 6-month forward projection timeline with monthly breakdown
  • BI (Business Intelligence) score with colour-coded band
  • Fragility Index score with severity classification

Manual Scenario Templates Starter+

Users on the Starter plan can manually select and run specific scenario templates. All paid plans above Starter get access to the full template library.

Available on Starter
  • Client Loss scenario
  • Payment Delay scenario
All scenario templates include
  • Custom scenario name
  • Parameter configuration (revenue change %, cost change %, timeline)
  • Product-specific scenario options (from catalogue)
  • 6-month simulation timeline output
  • Save simulation as a named decision

Business Intelligence (BI) Score

A composite 0-100 health score that classifies the business into performance bands with colour coding.

  • 80-100: Strong (green): healthy, growing business
  • 65-79: Stable (blue): solid fundamentals
  • 50-64: Caution (amber): visible stress signals
  • 30-49: At Risk (orange): action required
  • 0-29: Critical (red): urgent intervention needed

Business Fragility Index

Measures structural vulnerability across 6 dimensions, scored 0-100 where higher means more fragile.

Fragility Dimensions
  • Cash / runway fragility
  • Revenue predictability fragility
  • Customer concentration fragility
  • Operational dependency fragility
  • Capacity health fragility
  • Market exposure fragility
Fragility Levels
  • 0-20: Resilient (green)
  • 21-40: Stable with Exposure (blue)
  • 41-60: Fragile (amber)
  • 61-80: Highly Fragile (orange)
  • 81-100: Critical Fragility (red)

Simulation History & Decisions

  • Full history of all simulation runs stored per workspace
  • Each run stored with scenario name, parameters, and timeline output
  • Decision saving: mark a simulation as an accepted or rejected decision
  • Projection comparison (scenario vs do-nothing baseline)

Team Management & Permissions

Workspace owners can invite team members and control precisely what each member can see and do within the platform. Two permission models are supported.

Module-Level Permissions

  • Grant access to entire modules at once
  • Modules: Dashboard, Validation, Blueprints, Simulation, Catalogue, Financials, Registration
  • All features within a granted module are accessible
  • Fastest way to onboard a team member

Feature-Level Permissions

  • Granular control: grant specific features only
  • Example: grant "Invoices" only, not "Expenses" or "Contracts"
  • Example: grant "Run Simulation" without "Risk Detection"
  • Ideal for contractors or limited-role team members
Grantable features per module:
Dashboard: View Dashboard · Validation: Evaluate Idea, Service Idea Validation · Blueprints: Business Plan, Business Proposal, Sales Letter · Simulation: Run Simulation, Scenario Intelligence, Risk Detection, Recommendations · Catalogue: View Catalogue, Manage Products, Customers, Vendors · Financials: View Financials, Invoices, Quotations, Expenses, Contracts, Export Reports · Registration: Registration Guide, Company Name Check, SIC Code Lookup
Platform Feature

AI Credit System

AI-powered features consume credits from the user's credit balance. Credits can be topped up as needed. Every credit-consuming action is logged in the credit transaction history.

Features That Use Credits

  • Idea Validation (full run)
  • Market Data Refresh
  • Business Plan: Full generation
  • Business Plan: Section generation
  • Business Proposal: Full generation
  • Business Proposal: Section generation
  • Sales Letter: Full generation
  • Sales Letter: Section generation
  • Scenario Simulation run
  • Fragility Index AI interpretation
  • AI Field Suggestion ("AI Fill")

Credit Transaction Types

  • Allocation: monthly plan credit grant
  • Deduction: credit consumed by a feature
  • Hold: credits reserved for in-progress task
  • Hold Released: unreserved on task failure
  • Refund: returned on task failure
  • Expiry: unused monthly credits expired
  • Top-up: purchased additional credits
  • Admin Adjustment: manual admin change
  • Promotion: promotional credit grant
Explorer plan: 50 AI credits included as a one-time allocation. Starter plan: higher credit allocation with access to all AI features. Credit balance and full transaction history visible from the Credits page, with filters by feature, transaction type, and status.
Platform Feature

Referral Programme

Every registered user can enrol in the EnterprateAI Referral Programme. After accepting the programme terms, each user receives a unique referral link and code that can be shared to earn rewards.

How It Works

  • User enrols and accepts referral terms from the Referral page
  • Receives unique link: enterprate.ai/r/[CODE] and a short code
  • Shares link with potential subscribers
  • When a new user signs up and activates a paid plan via that link, the referrer earns 5%
  • Fraud detection and self-referral prevention are built in
  • Click tracking: each link click is recorded with referral code

Referral Dashboard (3 tabs)

  • Overview: total clicks, conversions, pending earnings
  • Transactions: full history of referral reward entries
  • Payout Profile: set bank transfer or PayPal details

Payout Options

  • Bank Transfer (BACS): account name, number, sort code
  • PayPal: PayPal email address
  • Admin payout queue: reviewed and approved by admin
  • Status tracking: Pending → Approved → Paid
