EnterprateAI Product Overview 
Product Summary 
EnterprateAI is a Business Intelligence and Decision Simulation SaaS platform 
designed to help micro-businesses validate ideas, structure their businesses correctly, 
and make safer operational decisions. 
The platform combines structured business modelling, deterministic financial analysis, 
and scenario-based stress testing to provide entrepreneurs with a clear understanding 
of the viability, stability, and fragility of their businesses. 
Unlike traditional business software that focuses on historical financial reporting, 
EnterprateAI provides forward-looking decision intelligence by modelling how business 
decisions affect financial health, operational capacity, and resilience to economic 
shocks. 
The system is designed specifically for micro-businesses and early-stage SMEs, which 
often lack access to structured decision-support tools.

Product Objectives 
EnterprateAI is designed to achieve three primary outcomes for entrepreneurs: 
1. Validate Business Ideas Before Launch 
Many entrepreneurs start businesses without structured validation. EnterprateAI 
provides a framework to analyse the viability of business ideas using unit economics, 
demand assumptions, and cost modelling. 
2. Provide Structured Business Planning 
Entrepreneurs often struggle to translate business ideas into structured plans. 
EnterprateAI converts validated business data into actionable business blueprints and 
operational documents. 
3. Improve Operational Decision Making 
Business owners regularly face decisions involving hiring, pricing, expansion, and cost 
management. EnterprateAI allows these decisions to be simulated before 
implementation, reducing risk. 
 
Product Architecture Overview 
The platform operates through three integrated modules aligned with the 
entrepreneurial lifecycle. 
Module Function 
Module 1 Idea Validation & Business Setup 
Module 2 Business Blueprint & Operational 
Documents 
Module 3 Financial & Operational Intelligence 
Each module builds upon structured data captured in the previous stage, creating a 
continuously improving intelligence model of the business.

Module 1: Idea Validation & Business Setup 
Module 1 helps entrepreneurs evaluate business ideas and establish their business 
structure correctly. 
This module is divided into three operational gates. 
 
Gate 1: Validate My Idea 
This stage supports users who want to test the viability of a new business idea or 
product/service concept. 
Users choose between two validation pathways: 
Business Idea Validation 
For entrepreneurs who do not yet have a registered business. 
The system collects structured inputs including: 
• problem statement 
• target customer segment 
• product or service offering 
• pricing model 
• demand assumptions 
• operational capacity 
• cost structure 
Using deterministic modelling, EnterprateAI calculates: 
• projected revenue 
• contribution margin 
• break-even threshold 
• delivery feasibility 
The platform then generates a Validation Score with actionable insights.

Product / Service Idea Validation 
For users who already operate a business but want to introduce a new service or 
product. 
In addition to the inputs above, the system also evaluates: 
• existing business revenue 
• operational capacity 
• current cost structure 
• potential operational conflicts 
This ensures the new offering does not destabilise the existing business. 
 
Gate 2: Register My Business 
For entrepreneurs whose idea has been validated but who have not yet registered their 
business. 
This module acts as a guided business formation companion. 
Features include: 
• company name availability checks using Company House APIs 
• SIC code recommendations (Company House API) 
• guidance on company formation requirements 
Users complete registration through the Companies House portal, while EnterprateAI 
ensures the system captures structured business data for future intelligence modelling.

Gate 3 Run My Business 
For entrepreneurs with an existing operating business. 
Users input or import structured business data including: 
• services and pricing 
• revenue streams 
• operational capacity 
• cost structures 
• client contracts 
• payment terms 
• available cash reserves 
This information forms the foundation of the platform’s intelligence engines. 
 
Module 2: Business Blueprint 
Module 2 transforms structured business data into practical business documents and 
operational frameworks. 
Using the validated business model, the platform generates: 
• Business Plans 
• Business Proposals 
• Sales Quotations 
• Basic Service Contracts 
• Basic Invoices 
The system uses large language models only for document generation, ensuring that all 
financial figures originate from deterministic calculations rather than AI estimation. 
This ensures consistency and reliability in generated documents.

Module 3: Financial & Operational Intelligence 
Module 3 provides ongoing decision intelligence for operating businesses. 
This module analyses business performance and models the potential outcomes of 
operational decisions. 
Three analytical engines power this module. 
 
Business Stability Engine 
The Stability Engine evaluates the structural health of the business based on: 
• liquidity 
• profitability 
• revenue stability 
• cost structure 
• operational capacity 
These factors produce a Business Stability Score (0–100) representing the overall 
strength of the business. 
 
Fragility Index Engine 
The Fragility Index measures how vulnerable the business is to disruptions. 
It simulates shocks such as: 
• revenue decline 
• cost increases 
• client loss 
• delayed payments 
The system measures how these shocks affect profitability and cash runway.

The resulting Fragility Index quantifies how easily the business can become financially 
unstable. 
Stress Testing Engine 
The Stress Testing Engine evaluates compound risk scenarios. 
Examples include: 
• market downturn combined with rising costs 
• loss of a major client combined with payment delays 
• hiring expansion during slow revenue growth 
These simulations allow founders to evaluate strategic decisions before implementation. 
 
Business Ontology and Intelligence Graph 
EnterprateAI uses a Business Ontology model to represent the structure of a business. 
Key entities include: 
• services and products 
• customers 
• revenue streams 
• operational resources 
• cost structures 
• contracts and invoices 
• financial state 
These entities form an Intelligence Graph where relationships between business 
elements are continuously analysed. 
Changes to one part of the business automatically propagate through the system.

• digital agencies
